--------------------------------
BLOCK G: POSTING LEGITIMACY
--------------------------------

Assess whether this posting is likely a real, active opening. This helps the
candidate prioritize effort.

Analyze these signals from the JD text and recent company signals:

1. Description quality
   - Does it name specific technologies, frameworks, or tools?
   - Does it mention team size, reporting structure, or org context?
   - Are requirements realistic? (years of experience vs technology age)
   - Is there clear scope for the first 6-12 months?
   - What ratio of the JD is role-specific vs generic boilerplate?

2. Requirements coherence
   - Do the requirements match the seniority level?
   - Any internal contradictions? (entry-level title + staff requirements, etc.)

3. Company hiring signals (from recent_signals)
   - Any recent layoffs in this department?
   - Is the company in active growth or contraction?
   - Does this role make sense for the company's current stage?

4. Compensation transparency
   - Is salary posted? (jurisdiction-dependent — low weight signal)

Rate the overall legitimacy:
- "High Confidence" — multiple signals suggest a real, active opening
- "Proceed with Caution" — mixed signals worth noting
- "Suspicious" — multiple ghost job indicators

Important: present observations, not accusations. Every signal has legitimate
explanations. The candidate decides how to weigh them.

Provide:
- legitimacy_tier: "High Confidence" | "Proceed with Caution" | "Suspicious"
- legitimacy_signals: list of objects with signal/finding/weight ("positive" | "neutral" | "concerning")
- legitimacy_notes: any caveats (niche role, government job, evergreen position, etc.)

FINAL SCORE

Compute a final deep_eval_score (1-5) that incorporates everything:

Final route:
